# Therapy Practice Risk Matrix

**Audit date:** 23 October 2026. This is a publication-risk screen, not a clinical protocol. No new instructions are authorized by this document. Existing practice sections remain locked.

## Classifications

- **A. CANDIDATE FOR EDUCATIONAL GUIDANCE** — a public patient-facing official source explicitly provides a bounded option or guidance; still requires source scope, safety and clinical review before publication.
- **B. REQUIRES PROFESSIONAL INSTRUCTION** — the source or technique depends on a professional/qualified person teaching the companion or user.
- **C. INSUFFICIENT SOURCE SUPPORT** — an exact definition, process, scope, safety note, or patient-facing instruction was not verified.
- **D. NOT SUITABLE FOR SELF-GUIDED INSTRUCTIONS** — the proposed instruction requires clinical assessment/supervision or is too consequential to turn into a public do-it-yourself sequence with current evidence.

## Criteria not yet met for any public practice sequence

- A single official patient-facing source with explicit intended population, labor stage or pregnancy context, and exact wording/steps.
- Clear source-level warnings and conditions (not inferred from other pages or a different therapy).
- Confirmation that steps do not require professional skill or assessment.
- Cross-source scope and conflict check, current URL/date check, and clinician review of the exact Indonesian adaptation.
- Separate rights review for copied text, diagrams, video, voice, music and audio recordings.

Therefore **no practice guide is cleared for activation in Phase 2.3**. This is a governance recommendation only; implementation and PWA publication/caching rules remain unchanged.
