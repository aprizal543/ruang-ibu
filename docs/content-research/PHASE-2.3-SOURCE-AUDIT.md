## Source-access limitations and corrections

- NICE NG235's recommendation text could be checked from NICE's indexed official page excerpt, but direct HTML retrieval returned 403. Findings above are limited to the explicit clauses captured; this is not a line-by-line audit of the complete NICE page.
- The Kemenkes 2024 PDF and S9/S10 warning-sign sources were not fully checked. None is used to validate the current T01–T04 claim set here.
- S6's relevant breathing section is verified, but publication date and specific reuse license were not exposed. Its concrete patient steps are a stronger candidate source for T01 labour education; the lack of a full warning set and its local service context mean it cannot alone clear a Ruang Ibu practice guide.
- S11 (draft URL) and S13 (NCCIH definition page) are separate pages. S11 does not establish the professional distinction used in Phase 2.2A's T02 editorial wording; S13 does. Phase 2.4 added S13 as a structured T02 reference and limited it to that general service-definition distinction; it does not support a pregnancy-specific outcome.
- The Cochrane evidence pages are open to read, not automatically open to reproduce. Keep source notes and summaries original.
