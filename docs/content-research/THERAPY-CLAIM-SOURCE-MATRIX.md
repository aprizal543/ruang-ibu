# Therapy Claim–Source Matrix

**Reviewed:** 23 October 2026. Scope is the current structured article content in `src/content/therapyArticles.ts` plus relevant content in `reference/MATERI-DRAF-v0.2.md`. This is evidence traceability, not a clinical approval.

## Status definitions

- **VERIFIED** — the source directly supports the narrowly worded claim in the specified population/context.
- **PARTIALLY SUPPORTED** — only a portion, population, intervention, outcome, or stage is supported.
- **UNSUPPORTED** — the inspected sources do not substantiate the claim as stated.
- **CONFLICTING** — sources give materially inconsistent information. No material conflict was found within the narrow claims below; key differences are scope differences and are marked partial.
